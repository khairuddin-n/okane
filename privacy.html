okane Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · updated 17 November 2026

okane is a web app for tracking daily finances. It runs entirely in your browser and has no server. The developer of okane cannot see, store, or collect your financial data.

What okane can access

  • okane asks for the Google drive.file permission. With it, okane can only create and open files that okane itself created (the "okane – Data" spreadsheet), never any other file in your Google Drive.
  • okane reads your Google account email address to show it on the Settings screen.

Where your data is stored

  • All records are stored in the "okane – Data" spreadsheet in your own Google Drive.
  • A copy is kept on your device (browser IndexedDB) so the app works offline. This copy is deleted when you sign out.
  • In local mode, data stays only on your device.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party. okane uses no ads, no analytics, and no external fonts or trackers.

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Revoke okane's access any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete your data by deleting the "okane – Data" file from your Google Drive.

okane's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
